# Fathom

A small light in an enormous dark. Slip into the flooded corridors of Fathom, a bioluminescent maze chase where every meal helps you see—and helps something else see you.

Graze the scattered plankton to grow your circle of vision. Sonar pours around corners, tracing passages and catching hidden hunters in cyan. The maze remembers everywhere you have explored, but the dark closes over anything beyond your window. Clear every mote, catch an amber drifter for a bonus, and descend.

Three predators read three different signals. A Lanternjaw follows your glow, wearing the same amber disguise as your next meal. The eyeless Gloamfin hears your pulse and sends one back. A Flarefish lights up the trench in a sudden bloom, seeing straight through the rock between you.

Let your light fade. Turn a corner. Leave a cloud of ink behind you. Each dive is a balance between learning what lies ahead and giving away where you are. The deeper you go, the more hunters join the maze—and the less your sonar can reach.
